How to read a Polymarket volume alert

A large increase in market volume tells you that more trading activity has been reported. It does not tell you that one informed trader made a large bet, or that the next price move will go in your favour.

Start with what was measured

The alert shown as 'Large volume change' (the former WHALE label, now corrected in the dashboard) is based on the difference between two observations of total market volume. Compare that difference with your configured threshold. The opt-in EXTREME browser notification still prefixes the old 'Whale' name; the measurement is the same volume change. The interval can be longer than the nominal polling schedule when a request fails or the browser pauses.

Open the underlying market

Read the full question, resolution rules and closing time. Check the latest price, available liquidity and market activity on the source platform. A short alert title cannot replace the market's own terms. If a source link does not open the intended market, stop and find the market on the source platform before relying on it.

Separate price direction from trade direction

A rising YES price does not prove that the whole volume increase came from one YES buyer. A falling price does not identify a NO whale. To describe a particular purchase, inspect trade-level evidence with a timestamp, outcome, size and transaction or wallet reference.

Use AI as a research aid

An AI explanation can suggest questions or possible links to other markets. Check its factual claims against original sources. Do not treat a fluent explanation as evidence of trader skill, a causal relationship or an investment edge.

Keep the evidence, including failures

For a worked example, record the alert time, source market, observed values and later checkpoints. Include alerts that did not precede a price move. One selected example cannot establish a hit rate or expected return.

This guide describes documented functionality from the product source. Six bounded feed checks between 13 and 15 November 2026 found only sports, geography and target-price NEW MARKET combination records, so no worked non-sports volume-change example is presented here. Nothing in this guide establishes a hit rate or an expected return.
