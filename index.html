Prediction-market activity, with context

PolyMind is a browser dashboard for watching prediction-market activity from Polymarket and Kalshi. It flags patterns such as large trades, price moves and volume changes, with AI-generated explanations to help you investigate what happened.

Use it to find a market worth checking, read the signal and compare it with the underlying market. An alert is a prompt for research, not evidence of an investment edge.

Short workflow

From a market to a question worth checking

  1. Open the free dashboard. Check the data-source status before relying on a market value.
  2. Browse Markets or wait for Alerts. Use the configuration thresholds to choose which activity matters to you.
  3. When an alert appears, open it to inspect the market context and request its AI explanation. Check labels, prices, dates and assumptions against the source market.
  4. Use History to revisit locally saved alerts. Treat AI output as an estimate, not a trading instruction.

Polymarket is checked about every 15 seconds and Kalshi about every 30 seconds while the page is visible and polling is active. Source latency and loading time can add delay. These are periodic checks, not an instant real-time feed.

What you can inspect

Keep the source and the explanation separate

Market activity
Browse markets and multiple alert categories, including large trades, volume changes and price moves.
AI context
An alert can trigger an AI explanation. Built-in AI uses Groq; optional Claude or Gemini access uses your own API key. AI output can be wrong.
History and filters
Watchlist filters and a local rolling alert archive help you revisit activity in the same browser. Clearing browser data can remove local history.
Research, not trade execution. PolyMind does not place trades. Its AI output can include suggested actions, prices and position sizes; those are generated estimates, not verified recommendations. Check important decisions independently and comply with market-access rules in your jurisdiction.
